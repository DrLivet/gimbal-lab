Gimbal Lab

This browser can't use Bluetooth. Open this page in Chrome on your Android phone or Mac.

1. Connect

Force-stop DJI Mimo first (Settings → Apps → DJI Mimo → Force stop). Gimbal unfolded, phone in the clamp, powered on.

2. Find the wake-up

Tap Run test and watch the gimbal. Each step tries one more thing Mimo does, turns the gimbal about 25° and back. If the page can read the gimbal's position it marks the result itself; otherwise tap Moved or Didn't move.

    3. Drive it

    Hold an arrow to move. Works once step 2 finds what wakes the gimbal.

    Advanced: send any command

    Explore any Bluetooth gimbal

    For the Feiyu SCORP-C: close the Feiyu app completely, power on the gimbal, then connect. The page lists everything the gimbal exposes and records everything it sends.
